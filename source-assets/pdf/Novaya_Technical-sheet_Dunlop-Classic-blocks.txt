Our long-established Dunlop vulcanisation technology guarantees
extraordinary quality for a truly exceptional sleep experience. The Dunlop
process is based on the principle of a steam oven, where latex is vulcanised at
a steady temperature of up to 100°C. Novaya perfected this method through
years of investment in R&D and maintaining high-quality standards. We
carefully select our raw materials to guarantee premium quality and comfort
characteristics. Our classic blend contains 20% natural latex,
straight from nature itself.
