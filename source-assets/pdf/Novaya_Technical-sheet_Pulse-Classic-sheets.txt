          Pulse
                                                               The most sustainable




The patented Pulse vulcanisation technology has been developed by Novaya
for improved ventilation and resilience. This latest innovation in comfort latex
technology draws inspiration from a microwave oven, or dielectric heating.
Here, radio waves create friction within the material, allowing vulcanisation
from the inside out. The result is an evenly vulcanised latex with improved
resilience and breathability. It’s also more energy efficient, making it the most
sustainable latex technology on the market. Our Classic blend contains a
balanced mixture of synthetic latex and natural latex.
